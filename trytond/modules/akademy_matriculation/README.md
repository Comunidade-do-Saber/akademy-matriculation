## 📘 About the Project

**SAGE Education** is a ***free and open-source*** project for ***educators, educational institutions, and governments***.

Its ***modular, scalable, and secure*** design allows it to be implemented in many different scenarios: from **small schools** and **training centers** to **large national public education systems**.

SAGE Education has a growing, committed, and friendly community that brings together the best in the fields of ***educational sciences, pedagogy, educational technology, and computer science***.
No matter where in the world you live, we are passionate about upholding education and educational technology as a non-negotiable human right.

## Akademy Matriculation Module

The **Akademy Matriculation (Enrollment Management)** module is a complementary module responsible for **managing school enrollments**, keeping all information organized and accessible to the institution.

It offers advanced tools for **enrollments, applications, and transfers**, allowing for centralized and efficient monitoring of each student's performance and progress.

### 🎯 Purpose

This module centralizes the **student enrollment lifecycle**, including:
- Registration and management of candidates,
- Submission and tracking of applications,
- Evaluation of applications according to criteria,
- Management of student transfers,
- Association of students to courses and disciplines,
- Generation of official enrollment and transfer reports.

It is designed for use by **schools, training centers, universities, and educational authorities**.

### ✨ Main Features

#### 📝 Candidate Management
- Registration of candidates
- Submission and management of applications
- Evaluation of applications based on criteria
- Reporting on candidates and application results

#### 🔄 Student Transfers
- Record internal and external student transfers
- Associate transferred students with courses and disciplines
- Generate transfer reports for institutional records

#### 🎓 Enrollment & Matriculation
- Wizard-based enrollment creation
- Association of students to courses and disciplines
- Management of enrollment references, phases, academic years, and levels

#### 📊 Reports & Documents
- Candidate reports
- Application results reports
- Enrollment and student transfer reports
- Equivalence discipline reports
- Criteria evaluation reports
